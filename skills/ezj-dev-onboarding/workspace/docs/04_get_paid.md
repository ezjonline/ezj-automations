# How You Get Paid

Ethan pays everyone in USD through Wise. **Wise to Wise is the fastest way by far:** it lands in minutes with no fees. Paying into a local bank works too, it's just slower and bounces more often.

## Option 1: a Wise account (try this first)

1. Sign up free at https://wise.com/register
2. Finish the ID check before your first project, not the day you get paid.
3. Fill in the payment details form: https://tally.so/r/yPOx7X (your full name exactly as on Wise, your Wise email, your Wisetag if you have one).

## Can I open a Wise account where I live?

The real test: try to sign up. If Wise lets you finish the ID check, use Option 1. As of September 2026:

- ✅ Works: US, UK, Europe, Canada, Australia, the Philippines, Singapore, Malaysia, South Africa, and more. India works in practice.
- ❌ Not open to residents, use Option 2: Nigeria, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Kenya, Ghana, Egypt, Indonesia, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Uganda.
- 🚫 Blocked completely: Afghanistan, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, Iraq, Libya, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, Somalia, Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, Yemen. Tell Ethan and you'll work out another way.

## Option 2: your local bank or mobile money

Ethan sends from Wise to your account in your local currency, usually in 1 to 2 working days. Wise needs every field or the payment bounces, so copy them straight from your banking app.

Everyone sends: full legal name exactly as on the account (English letters), personal or business account, and your address (street, city, postcode, country). Plus:

| Country | What to send |
|---|---|
| Nigeria | Bank name and your 10 digit account number |
| Pakistan | IBAN (starts with PK, 24 characters) and bank name |
| India | Bank account number and IFSC code. Not UPI |
| Kenya | M-PESA phone number, or bank name and account number |
| Bangladesh | Bank name, branch name and account number, or bKash number |
| Ghana | Mobile money number and provider, or bank name and account number |
| Egypt | IBAN (starts with EG, 29 characters) |
| Philippines | Use Wise. Otherwise bank or e-wallet (GCash, Maya) and account number |
| Anywhere else | Tell Ethan your country and he'll send the exact fields |

## Send your details

Fill in https://tally.so/r/yPOx7X once. Only Ethan sees it. **Never post bank details in Slack, WhatsApp or a GitHub comment.** Changed banks? Fill it in again.

## When you get paid

- Your fee is in the kickoff message for each project.
- The payment reference names the project.
- Nothing after 3 working days? One message in your project thread with the date and the amount. No bank details in the message.
- You're an independent contractor, so taxes where you live are on you.
