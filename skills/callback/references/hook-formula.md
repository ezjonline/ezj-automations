# The hook formula

What made a call reel hit ~100k views and 1,090 comments, turned into a scoring rubric.

The reel: a prospect watching a live demo of an AI agent. It opened on the prospect saying "this is sick", cut back to the start of the demo, ran white sticker boxes narrating their reaction every few seconds, and ended right before "this is sick" so the replay looped straight into it. The caption said comment a keyword and I'll show you how.

## Why call reels work

A scripted reel is you telling people it works. A call reel is somebody else finding out it works, live, on camera. The viewer watches the guest's face, not the demo. The reaction is the content.

## Score every window 1 to 10

Read the transcript in 20 to 60 second windows. Score each on four things, then average:

| | 10 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaction** | The guest audibly reacts: "this is sick", "wait, what", "take my money", laughing, swearing | Nobody reacts, or it is all the host talking |
| **Stakes** | A number, a cost, a result, a before and after ("that's 20 hours a week") | Vague talk about process |
| **Clarity** | A stranger with zero context gets it in 3 seconds | Needs the previous ten minutes to make sense |
| **Standalone** | Starts and ends on its own, no "like I said earlier" | Pronouns with nothing to point at |

Keep 7 and up. Below 9, only keep windows with a real guest reaction. Those are the keepers.

## The cold open

1.5 to 6 seconds, the most arresting line in the window, from ANY speaker. Replayed at the very front before the moment runs from its real start.

Ranked best first:
1. A genuine reaction ("this is fucking sick", "take my money", "no way")
2. A number or a receipt ("we booked 14 calls off that")
3. A flat contrarian claim ("you don't need more leads")
4. A named cost or outcome ("that's a full salary gone")
5. A hard question ("why are you still doing this by hand?")

Read it with nothing in front of it. If it does not land with zero setup, it is not the cold open.

Disqualified as a cold open: lines that start with "so", "and", "basically", "I think", "yeah", or a pronoun with nothing to point at. **If no line in the window clears the bar, drop the window.**

## The loop

When the cold open line sits in the last ~10 seconds of the window, end the reel right before it. The replay runs straight back into the cold open and a chunk of viewers watch twice without noticing. Otherwise let the moment land naturally; hearing the cold open twice is fine and on purpose.

## Stickers

3 to 6 per reel, one every ~5 seconds, held ~3 seconds. White box, bold black text. They narrate the guest's reaction arc, like a friend leaning over going "watch this".

- First sticker is the title card, at 0 seconds, held 4: "Taking them from confused to 'take my money' in 30 secs" or your own version of it.
- Then the arc: "Wait for their reaction", "Wait for it...", "It just clicked", "Now FOMO sets in...", "Starting to see how this scales", "SOLD".
- The guest is they or them on screen. Never a name.
- Zone `bottom` for lines about the guest's reaction, `center` or `top` for lines about what is being shown.

## Layout

| The call has | Layout | Zones |
|---|---|---|
| Both cameras on | halves | you 960 on top, guest 960 on the bottom |
| A screen share that shows the thing being discussed | stack | you 600, share 720, guest 600 |
| A guest who has not agreed to be shown | faces hidden | you 960 on top, share 960 on the bottom |

A share in stack fills its band. Blur it (`"blur": 4`) if anything private is legible.

## The caption

The CTA goes in the post caption, not the video. One line of hook, then: comment a keyword and I'll send you the thing. A comment keyword turned a single reel into 1,090 comments.

## What to skip

- Windows where only the host talks and nobody reacts
- Anything with a client's private numbers you cannot blur away
- Team meetings, internal handoffs, family calls
- Moments where the grid reshuffles mid-window and the faces jump around
