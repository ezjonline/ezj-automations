# Drafting quality and reuse rules

How a drafted overlay must read, and when to reuse, draft, or flag. The example lines below are
illustrative, made up to show a shape, not anyone's real overlays.

## Drafting quality (for any NEW overlay)

- **`Text on screen:` label** is a 2 to 4 word distilled NAME for the beat ("Vary your angles", "Cut the
  pause"), often an imperative or a principle. It never repeats the spoken line back.
- **`[director note]`** is a concrete "show X" that names the exact visual: the specific screen
  recording, the specific tool, the specific shot ("PUT PHOTO 3 + 4 next to each other"). Never "show
  relevant b-roll" or "add a graphic here".
- **`Make an overlay` toggle** carries the save-worthy payload: the list, the steps, the exact numbers,
  the filming spec. Nest shot direction inside items: "Walking in [film at 0.5x]", "Close up on the
  cup [2x zoom]".
- **Be specific and numeric.** When a spoken line has a number, a tool, or a setting, that is the
  overlay. Pull the exact values from the script verbatim.
- **Imperative and practical.** The viewer should be able to DO the thing from the overlay alone.
- **The overlay must add what the words can't.** If it would just subtitle a line the viewer already
  hears, it does not belong.
- Voice: from `references/brand-brain/voice.md`, Register C. Default to second person, contractions,
  one idea per unit, calm and specific, the creator's own list marker. No dashes as punctuation.

**Auto-reject:** restating the spoken line as a label; vague director notes ("show b-roll", "insert
graphic"); vague list items (nouns with no number or shot direction); hype or adjectives in a label; any
invented number, brand, tool, asset, or setting the creator did not say; pressure framing; two adjacent
near-identical overlays that should be merged.

## Reuse vs draft vs flag (decide per placement, in this order)

**REUSE from the live overlay bank.** The bank is the Notion page (and sub-pages) linked in
`references/brand-brain/examples.md`. Read it live every run and search for entries relevant to this
script's topics. Because it is read live, anything new the creator adds is picked up automatically.
Rules:
- Reuse only entries genuinely relevant to THIS script. **Never repeat the whole bank in one video.**
  Most videos reuse a few relevant entries at most.
- Prefer reuse over drafting when the bank already covers a point well. That is the time saver.
- The result should be a **mix**: some reused, some freshly drafted for points the bank does not cover.
- A design tool library (page numbered templates, presets) is usually NOT browsable. Only name such an
  asset if it appears in this row or the bank. Otherwise draft or flag.

If no overlay bank link is set, skip reuse, draft or flag everything, and say in the report that
adding a bank link would save time on every run.

**DRAFT NEW** when the content is fully derivable from the spoken line, the row context, or the
creator's known topics, with zero external facts. Example: the script lists a sequence of shots aloud,
and you structure it into the toggle with shot directions.

**FLAG** (write no content, point at the script line) when the overlay would need a fact you do not
have (a real statistic, brand, tool, price, or metric the creator did not state), or the script
gestures at a personal asset ("my template", "the settings I use") that is not in this row or the bank.
Write a flagged candidate like `[reuse: do you have a saved graphic for this?]` or `[reference your own
template here]` rather than inventing.

**Hard invention bans (never, any branch):** never invent a design tool page number, an animation or
preset name, an asset reference, a statistic, a view count, or a brand or tool the creator did not name.
When reuse would need a name you cannot confirm, downgrade to FLAG. A flag is always safer than an
invention.
