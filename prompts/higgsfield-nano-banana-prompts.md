# 3 realistic AI photo prompts (Higgsfield, Soul 2.0)

Free prompts, no catch. These are the exact ones I used to generate a set of hyper-realistic AI photos of myself with Higgsfield's Soul 2.0 model. Paste them straight in.

## How to use these

1. In Higgsfield, train a Soul 2.0 character on 15 to 25 photos of yourself (different angles, different lighting, at least one full-height shot). This is what keeps your face and body consistent across every generation instead of drifting.
2. Pick a prompt below, paste it into the `soul_2` model with your trained `soul_id` attached.
3. Set aspect ratio to `3:4`. Soul 2.0 doesn't have a native 4:5 option yet, and 3:4 is the closest to a normal Instagram post. If you need exact 4:5, crop it after.
4. Generate. Expect to run a couple of variants per prompt, not every one lands first try.

## The prompts

### 01. Gas station

```
Candid flash-lit street portrait at a 7-Eleven gas station, 1:47 AM, twenty minutes after rain. A young man stands square to camera in front of a glossy tangerine-orange JDM sports car with an oversized rear wing, its clearcoat beaded with water and warped with reflections of the canopy fluorescents. He wears a fitted black ribbed tank top, creased leather pants, a chunky statement belt crowded with enamel pins, and layered gold Cuban chains that blow out to pure white where the flash catches them. Mid-laugh: smirking, one eye winked shut, tongue out, both hands thrown up in a playful double middle finger, his left hand caught in the faintest motion blur while his right is frozen razor-sharp by the flash.
Shot on a Canon EOS-1V, 35mm f/1.4, direct on-camera flash at f/8, CineStill 800T. Harsh frontal flash carving a hard-edged shadow onto the fuel pump behind him, light falling off into dense black at the frame edges. Red halation blooming off the neon signage and the taillights of a car streaking past in the background. Wet asphalt mirroring magenta, cyan, and sodium-vapor amber into long vertical smears. Moths swarming the canopy lights. Steam curling off a manhole across the street.
Hyper-real skin: visible pores, sweat sheen on collarbones, faint stubble, a few flyaway hairs lit up by the flash. Slight chromatic aberration at the corners, a dust speck on the negative, a soft light leak bleeding orange along the right edge, subtle 800-speed grain in the shadows. Amateur-candid framing, not studio-perfect. Mood: cocky, kinetic, gloriously unserious. --style raw
```

### 02. Milk and cereal

```
A nighttime street scene in front of a liquor store, lit by harsh flash. A young man pours a full gallon of milk over his head, milk splashing down his face. He wears an oversized white t-shirt soaked with liquid. In his other hand, he holds an overflowing colorful cereal bowl, milk and cereal spilling dramatically toward the camera. Wide-angle perspective, chaotic motion freeze, gritty urban backdrop, neon signage above, high contrast, wet textures, surreal and rebellious street-editorial mood. --cinematic flash lighting --high detail --style raw
```

### 03. Desert handshake

```
Candid flash-lit portrait in the New Mexico desert at dusk, twenty feet from a beat-up silver RV with a cracked windshield, sagebrush and red mesa stretching into the background under a bruised purple sky. He stands center-frame in a plain white tee, sleeves pushed up, locked in a firm handshake with a man built like a classic meth-cook TV villain: bald head, thin mustache, round wire-frame glasses, tan bucket hat, an oversized yellow hazmat suit unzipped to the waist with the hood hanging back, a cigarette pinched between two fingers of his free hand. Both men lock eyes and grin like they just closed a deal, grip tight, knuckles white, a duffel bag of loose cash spilling a few bills onto the dirt at their feet. Shot on a Canon EOS-1V, 35mm f/1.4, direct on-camera flash at f/8, CineStill 800T, the flash blows out both faces and throws hard double shadows onto the RV behind them, the desert falling into flat black past fifteen feet. Fine dust kicked up and frozen mid-air by the flash, heat shimmer just visible at the horizon, a few gnats caught glowing in the beam. Hyper-real skin texture, sweat sheen, slight motion blur in the clasped hands while both faces stay razor sharp. Slight chromatic aberration at the corners, faint 800-speed grain in the shadows, amateur-candid framing, not studio-perfect. Mood: absurd, cocky, movie-poster energy played completely straight. --style raw
```

## Why these work

Every one of them follows the same structure: a specific real location, not a vague setting. A loud, physical prop doing the scroll-stopping. A camera and film stock called out by name, which pushes the model toward photographic realism instead of the smooth "AI look." And a mood word that reads as unbothered, never posed. Swap the location, wardrobe, and prop, keep that structure, and you've got your own.
