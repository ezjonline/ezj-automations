# Realistic AI photo prompts (Nano Banana / Higgsfield safe)

Free prompts, no catch. These are hyper-realistic AI photo prompts built in the "gritty flash photo" style, direct on-camera flash, real film grain, one loud prop doing the scroll-stopping. Copy, paste, swap in your own reference photos.

## How to use these

1. Have 1 to 3 clear reference photos of your face ready (front-facing, decent lighting).
2. Paste a prompt below into any image model that accepts a reference photo (Nano Banana, Higgsfield, Midjourney with a character reference, etc.) and attach your photo(s) as the reference image.
3. Each prompt already has a face-lock instruction built in, telling the model to preserve your face exactly. Don't skip it, it's what keeps you looking like you and not a random AI face.
4. Aspect ratio 4:5 is the Instagram-native carousel size. Not every model offers it natively, 3:4 is the closest fallback if 4:5 isn't an option.

## Why these work

Every one of them follows the same structure: a specific real location, not a vague setting. A loud, physical prop doing the scroll-stopping. A camera and film stock called out by name where relevant, which pushes the model toward photographic realism instead of the smooth "AI look." A face-lock instruction that keeps identity intact. And a mood word that reads as unbothered, never posed. Swap the location, wardrobe, and prop, keep that structure, and you've got your own.
